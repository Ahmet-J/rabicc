About us

About Al Rabiic General Trading

Connecting quality automotive products with businesses across Somaliland.

Al Rabiic General Trading is an importation and distribution company serving the Somaliland market. The company specializes in distributing Linglong Tires and Dongah Batteries to retailers and businesses supporting the automotive and transportation sectors.

Our product offering addresses a broad range of automotive requirements, from passenger vehicles and family cars to commercial transportation, trucks, buses, and other vehicle applications.

We are committed to building dependable business relationships, supporting retailers, and providing access to automotive products that meet the needs of our market.
